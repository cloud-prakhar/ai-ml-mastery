# Image prompt — CLIP Contrastive Learning

**Purpose:** show images and captions matched in one embedding space, and classification by the closest caption
**Audience:** 🟡 intermediate
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (documentation) / 9:16 (learning card)
**Teaches:** [09 Computer Vision — Detectors, Vision Transformers, SAM and CLIP](../../09-computer-vision/06-detectors-vit-sam-and-clip.md)

## Prompt

A friendly, modern, flat-illustration diagram explaining contrastive image and text learning.
Style: clean vector, high contrast, generous whitespace, rounded shapes,
soft shadows, educational infographic aesthetic, minimal text.
Palette: blue #2563eb, amber #d97706, purple #a21caf, green #059669 on a light background.
Show: on the left, a column of three simple pictures (a dog, a bicycle, a cup) and a column of three short caption
cards. In the centre, a 3-by-3 grid where the three diagonal squares are bright green and the other six are pale.
On the right, a single new picture of a dog with arrows to three caption cards, the arrow to the dog caption thick
and green. Connect the parts with clear arrows. Label each element with two or three words maximum.
No photorealism. No clutter. No small print. No fake logos or brand marks.

## Text overlay (keep it this short)
- Title: Match Pictures to Words
- Labels: "Images", "Captions", "Match the diagonal", "Closest caption"

## Accuracy checks before use
- [ ] The bright squares are on the diagonal: each image with its own caption
- [ ] The classification panel picks the caption, not a trained class label
- [ ] Any text in the image is spelled correctly
- [ ] Nothing implies endorsement by a real company
