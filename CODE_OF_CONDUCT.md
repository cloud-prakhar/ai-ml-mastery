# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This repository exists to help people learn something difficult. We pledge to make participation
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Encouraged:**
- Answering beginner questions without condescension. Everyone was a beginner; most of us still are
  in some area of this repository.
- Giving specific, actionable feedback on content.
- Accepting correction gracefully when you are wrong.
- Assuming good faith when someone's explanation differs from yours.

**Unacceptable:**
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Dismissing questions as stupid, obvious, or "just google it".
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when representing the
project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a confidential issue or contacting the maintainers
directly. Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect
the reporter's privacy.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for
behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
