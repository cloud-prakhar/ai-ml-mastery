# 04. Artificial Intelligence Foundations

**Level:** 🟢 Beginner &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Effort:** Short module &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Status:** 📋 Backlog

What 'intelligence' and 'artificial intelligence' actually mean, how the field arrived here, and how AI, ML, deep learning and Generative AI relate.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this module you will be able to:

- Draw the containment relationship between AI, ML, DL and Generative AI.
- Distinguish automation from learning systems.
- Summarise the history of AI including both AI winters.
- Describe symbolic AI and why it gave way to statistical learning.

_None - this module can be started at any time._
